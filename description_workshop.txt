The Ancients have Awakened. Chaos reigns, storms ravage, and reality tears at the seams!

Reexperience the original 1.3 version of Ancients Awakened; ported to 1.4.4.

Notice: Multiplayer is currently very buggy, and known to corrupt worlds. Play at your own risk!

Looking for a new, modern take on the original mod? Check out [url=steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3596943461] Ancients Awakened[/url]!

Want to report issues or just want to talk about the mod? Check out our [url=discord.gg/ghChaEa] Discord[/url]!

Ancients Awakened: Classic features the original mod's:
[list]
[*] 800+ Items
[*] 100+ NPCs
[*] 21 Bosses (7 Pre-Hardmode, 4 Hardmode, and 10 Post-Moon Lord)
[*] 4 New Biomes:
[list]
[*] The Inferno: a volcanic biome ravaged by dragons, with gear mainly focusing on raw defense.
[*] The Mire: a swampy biome infested by slithering creatures of the dark, with gear mainly focusing on sheer speed.
[*] The Void: Floating Islands at the top right of the world. Nothing is there, there is no reason to go there (Or is there?)
[*] The Terrarium: A crystal biosphere safe from the ever-corrupting world around you.
[/list]
[*] Cross-Mod content with Thorium
[*] Fargo's Mutant Mod and Calamity Mod support
[*] Unique soundtrack
[/list]

Additionally, Ancients Awakened: Classic features new World Generation options to add additional content, both old and new, to a world:
[list]
[*] Removed: Brings back older content from previous versions of the mod that were removed over the course of development:
[list]
[*] Techno Truffle: A cybernetic mushroom menace created by the enigmatic Fulgarians, it combines moves from both the Mushroom Monarch and Feudal Fungus.
[*] The Siege Mechs: This trio of bosses fought around the same time as the vanilla Mechanical Bosses,
[list]
[*] The Retriever: A cybernetic Grip of Chaos that can move at high speeds.
[*] Orthrus X: A cybernetic version of the Hydra wielding high-power lasers.
[*] Raider Ultima: A cybernetic version of the Broodmother armed with devastating firepower.
[/list]
[/list]
[*] Unreleased: Features new content never-before-seen in public versions of the mod. This content has been pulled from in-dev versions of Ancients Awakened:
[list]
[*] The Lost Keep: An underground citadel which was once the home of a legendary hero, it holds a slew of mysteries to be uncovered.
[list]
[*] The Biomite Core: The Lost Keep's defence mechanism which wields powers from every biome, it can be challenged by offering a Biome Prism.
[/list]
[*] Anubis; Legendscribe: Anubis has been reworked with an all new fight! Only by paying careful attention to the runes he wields will you emerge victorious.
[*] Infinity Zero: Face the Void's Superancient, commanding 6 Zero Units and harboring an even more malicious presence.
[*] The Soul of Cthulhu: Awaken the Ocean's Superancient which lurks within the new Sunken Ship structure. It calls upon fearsome recreations of previous bosses.
[/list]
[*] Unofficial: Includes a handful of QoL changes, visual enhancements, and more created by the porting team to enhance the mod's experience:
[list]
[*] Boss Visual Improvements: Many bosses have been given visual enhancements to make them feel more alive and dynamic.
[*] Anubis Quest UI: Anubis' boss dialogue has been expanded into a full Quest UI, making it easier to follow along.
[*] And More!
[/list]
[/list]

Finally, Ancients Awakened: Classic aims to offer full compatibility with the new Ancients Awakened, disabling classic content that overlaps with new content to facilitate a smooth, complete playthrough with both enabled.

Note: Some of the mod's tracks have been removed or replaced at the request of their original composers. Please respect their decision.
