after that is item and npc priority. everything suffixless is either an item or an npc, and anything that isnt 
gets marked as what they are as a suffix

proj the proj would be named EnchantedSword_Proj)

files are organized primarily based on world completion (_PreHarmode, _Hardmode, and _PostMoonlord)
anything obtained then goes under there
WITH THE EXCEPTION OF WORLDGEN, anything genned on worldgen that isnt an ore goes under World in the primary class
anything used by multiple npcs/items goes under the root theme folder as well, tho u should avoid that and maybe
rework shit so that this doesnt happen but thats up to u
so yea. all biomes, worldgen, buffs, quest items go under the root class, and all items and npcs go under the
progression points
